## Overview

A nostalgic reimagining of the classic water-filled ring toss toy. Players use air bubbles to propel rings onto pegs, featuring physics-based movement and challenging levels.

## Contributions

- Implemented physics-based buoyancy and water resistance mechanics.
- Developed responsive bubble-pushing controls for mobile and desktop.
- Designed multiple levels with increasing difficulty and obstacles.
- Integrated sound effects and visual feedback for ring captures.

## Outcome

- Successfully published a polished web-based version on itch.io.
- Achieved a satisfying mechanical feel that (almost fully) mimics real-world physics.
